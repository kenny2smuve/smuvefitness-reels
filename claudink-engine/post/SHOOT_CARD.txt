Before the needle: the design ON THE BODY (print held on the skin, stencil, iPad), their first reaction.
Say the numbers out loud, in the story: how many hours, how many sessions, where they came from, the price.
One pain question and the answer ("would you tap?", "top three?").
One joke or one line of banter. Keep it.
At least 4 needle-on-skin shots across the session, from your eyeline.
At the end: "we're done" or "go check it out", the mirror, the client reacting, approval in their words.
Camera in hand ("wow, that's the one", posing directions), then the finished piece held 3+ seconds in natural light.
If they come back: a healed revisit with a joke before the reveal, every number spoken again.
Never: a TV or screen in frame, a color piece, a still client with only linework showing, blurry walking shots.
