Questions

What do I get for $9.99 a month?

Kenny's full plan on your phone: 16 workouts mapped over 8 weeks, every exercise animated, sets, reps and swaps planned, and a place to log your sets, weight and rest. A fresh block lands every two weeks.

What happens after I pay?

Checkout takes you straight into your plan on that phone. Kenny's welcome email also brings a sign-in link, so you can open it again any time from Member access.

Is this a monthly subscription? Can I cancel?

Yes. It's $9.99 USD a month and renews until you cancel. Cancel anytime from Member access before your renewal date to stop the next charge. You keep access until the paid month ends.

Men's or Women's plan?

The Men's plan is built for full-body strength and size: Push, Pull, Legs and Full Body. The Women's plan puts more focus on glutes, legs and lower-body strength, with less pressing. Pick the one that fits your goals.

How often do the workouts change?

Every two weeks. The main lifts stay the same so you can track progress. The rest changes.

Training Club or 1-on-1 coaching?

The Training Club is Kenny's workouts, done on your own. Coaching adds a plan built for you, plus check-ins with Kenny in Miami, Broward or online.
