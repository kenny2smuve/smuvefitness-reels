Progressive overload: how to keep getting stronger

Progressive overload just means asking your body to do a little more over time. It is the main reason a good plan keeps working.

01

Three simple ways to do it

  • Add repsSame weight, one more rep per set.
  • Add weightOnce you hit the top of your rep range on every set, go up a small step next session.
  • Add sets or controlOne extra set, or slower, cleaner reps with the same weight.
02

Double progression

Pick a rep range, for example 8 to 12. Start at a weight where you can do 8. Each session try to add a rep. When you reach 12 on all sets, increase the weight slightly and start back near 8.

03

Why logging matters

You cannot beat last week if you do not know what last week was. Writing down sets, weight, reps and rest turns every workout into a target. The SMUVE Training Club lets you log them right on your phone.

04

Common mistakes

  • Jumping weight too fastand losing form.
  • Changing every exercise every weekso there is nothing to compare.
  • Skipping rest days and sleep

Quick answers

How fast should I add weight?

Small steps, only after you reach the top of your rep range on every set with good form.

What if I cannot add weight?

Add a rep, add a set, slow the tempo or tighten your form. Any of these is progress.
