Quick answers

How much is the Men's plan?

$9.99 per month, billed monthly, cancel anytime.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The free sample has real animated exercises with set tracking and needs no card.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It opens in your phone's browser and can be added to your home screen like an app.

Is there a women's version?

Yes. The Women's plan puts more focus on glutes, legs and lower-body strength.

How do I cancel?

Any time, in two taps: Member access → Manage membership opens your billing portal. Your plan stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

What equipment do I need?

A normal gym: barbell, dumbbells, cables and the usual machines. Every lift lists a swap if a machine is taken.

Is it OK for beginners?

Yes. Block 1 teaches the four sessions at a lighter effort (RPE 8), every exercise has an animated demo with form cues, and the sets, reps, rest and weights are written out for you.
