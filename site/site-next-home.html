Questions

Training Club or coaching?+

The Training Club is my workouts, done on your own. Coaching adds a plan built for you, plus check-ins with me.

How often do the workouts change?+

Every two weeks. The main lifts stay the same so you can track progress. The rest changes.

Is this a monthly subscription?+

Yes. The Training Club is $9.99 USD a month and renews until you cancel. Cancel before your renewal date to stop the next charge. You keep access until the paid month ends.

Do I need coaching to start?+

No. Join the Training Club on its own, or ask about coaching when you’re ready.

What happens after I join?+

Log in to Member access with the email you used at checkout. Your current block, billing and cancel options are all there.
